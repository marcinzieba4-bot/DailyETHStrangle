Sell the Saturday straddle, buy the Sunday one

Every weekend, sell a one-day ETH at-the-money straddle on Saturday morning and buy one on Sunday morning. The Saturday sale is sized up a little after losses and gets a one-time perp hedge if ETH moves 3%. This page shows every rule, how each position is sized, what happened on all weekends since March 2021, and charts of the results.

What to take from this

  • The edge is the weekend calendar, not daily vol in general. Saturday options are priced for more movement than Saturdays deliver, and Sunday options for less than Sundays deliver, because Monday's 08:00 expiry carries the weekend gap into the Asian/European open. Other weekdays showed no edge.
  • Sunday made most of the money () though it wins only of the time. Saturday added with a hit rate. Together they offset each other's bad weekends.
  • The 3% hedge is close-to-free insurance. Its net result was in total, yet the yearly return is about the same with it () as without (). It cut the max drawdown from to and the worst single day from to .
  • Recovery sizing is gentle: at most 1.5× the account, and only on Saturdays. It was active on of Saturdays and adds about a year over fixed 1× sizing.
  • Expect flat years. 2023, 2024 and 2026 so far returned , and , and nearly all the profit came in 2021, 2022 and 2025. A random 2-year resample of the history looks kinder (median /yr, only of paths lost money) because it mixes good and bad stretches. Plan for a year that makes nothing.
  • How to use it: start at 0.5× the account for 8–12 weekends to check that your real fills match the 1.5% haircut assumed here, then move to 1×. The backtest is a USD account, so sell the wstETH first or short 62.45 ETH of perp against it before trading.

The rules, in order of the weekend

All times are UTC (your local time shown underneath). Deribit daily options expire at 08:00 UTC, so a Saturday option lives exactly 24 hours, and so does a Sunday option. One contract = 1 ETH.

  1. Sat 08:00Sell the ATM call and put expiring Sunday 08:00. Size = account × (1 + recovery), max 1.5×.
  2. Sat 09:00 → Sun 05:00every hourIf ETH is 3% or more from the strike, open a perp hedge once: size = straddle delta × contracts.
  3. Sun 06:00Close the hedge (if open) 2 hours before expiry.
  4. Sun 08:00Saturday straddle settles. Buy the ATM call and put expiring Monday 08:00, size = account × 1.
  5. Mon 08:00Sunday straddle settles. Flat until next Saturday.

1Saturday: sell the straddle

At 08:00 UTC sell the call and the put at the listed strike nearest to ETH, expiring Sunday 08:00. Use limit orders a little under the mark. The model assumes you get mark − 1.5% and pay a 0.03% fee per leg.

Why: Saturday's options are priced for more movement than Saturdays deliver. A seller kept about of the premium on average.

2Saturday: hedge once at 3%

Check the hourly close. The first time ETH is ≥3% above or below the strike, trade the perp against the straddle's delta: short perp after a fall, long perp after a rise. Hold that fixed size, no re-balancing, and close it at Sunday 06:00. At most one hedge per Saturday.

Why: a 3% move in a few hours is the start of most of the large Saturday losses. Locking in the delta at that point stops a trend from doubling the loss. A small move that reverses costs a little.

3Saturday: recovery sizing

If the account is below its previous high, add extra size so that its premium alone repays 1/8 of the gap. Total size is capped at 1.5× the account. Back at a new high, return to 1×.

Why: it speeds up getting back to the high without martingale risk. Eight good Saturdays clear the gap, and the 1.5× cap keeps a bad run survivable.

4Sunday: buy the straddle

At 08:00 UTC, right after the Saturday option settles, buy the ATM call and put expiring Monday 08:00, with notional equal to the account (1×). No hedge and no recovery: hold to expiry. The model assumes you pay mark + 1.5% plus fees.

Why: Sunday options are priced as if Sunday were as quiet as Saturday. But the Monday 08:00 expiry includes Sunday evening and the Asia open, when the weekend's news gets priced. The buyer made about over the premium paid.

Why it works: premium vs. the move that followed

A straddle pays the absolute move from the strike to expiry. So the seller's edge is premium − |move| and the buyer's edge is |move| − premium. These figures are net of the 1.5% haircut and fees, as a % of notional, averaged per weekend.

How each position is sized

Below are the formulas, worked through with the numbers from the most recent weekend in the data, . The trade inspector further down does the same for any weekend.

ASaturday: how many contracts to sell

net per ETH = (call + put) × (1 − 1.5%) − fees
rate = net per ETH ÷ ETH price
extra = (previous high − account) ÷ (8 × rate)
notional = min(account + extra, 1.5 × account)
contracts = notional ÷ ETH price
In words: the extra notional is the amount whose premium alone pays back one eighth of the drawdown. In this weekend that would have been far more than the account, so the 1.5× cap applied. That was the case on of all Saturdays.

BHedge: how much perp to trade

trigger: |ETH ÷ strike − 1| ≥ 3% on an hourly close (Sat 08:00 to Sun 05:00)
hedge ETH = (Δcall + Δput) × contracts
Δ from Black-Scholes at the same IV with the hours left to expiry
positive → buy perp · negative → sell perp
close all at Sun 06:00
Δcall + Δput is the straddle's delta per contract: 0 at the strike, and close to ±1 once ETH is several percent away with only hours left. The hedge sets the position's net delta back to zero at that moment and then is left alone.

CSunday: how many contracts to buy

cost per ETH = (call + put) × (1 + 1.5%) + fees
notional = account after Saturday (1×)
contracts = notional ÷ ETH price
max loss = contracts × cost per ETH
No recovery on Sunday. The same rule on the Sunday leg (8 weekends, cap 1.5×) lifts the return to /yr but deepens the max drawdown to . A leg that wins less than half the time is a poor one to size up after losses.

Margin: under Deribit standard margin a short ATM straddle needs roughly 15% of the index per leg plus the option's mark, about 32% of notional. At the 1.5× cap that is about 48% of the account, plus margin for the perp hedge. The long Sunday straddle is fully paid, and its premium is the most you can lose. Sizing is in USD: in an ETH-margined account, hedge the ETH collateral with a perp short or the results also carry the ETH price.

Risks and what the backtest cannot see

  • Fills are the biggest unknown. The model assumes you sell 1.5% under the mark and buy 1.5% over it. With fills at the mark the result would be /yr, at 3% from mark /yr, and at 5% /yr. Across all trades in next-day options, the median gap between trade price and mark was on Saturdays and on Sundays. That figure is inflated by cheap out-of-the-money options and mark lag, but it is why the first weeks should log every fill against the mark.
  • Capacity. At $1M the trade is ~500 ETH per leg. That is fine on Deribit's daily expiries most weekends, but it is a large share of a quiet Saturday's volume. Above ~$2–3M, split the orders or use a block trade.
  • Strike rounding. The model sets the strike exactly at the 08:00 price. Real strikes sit on a grid, so ETH can be a fraction of a percent away. At 60% IV, 0.6% away is a straddle delta of about ±0.17 per contract. Offset it with the perp at entry to match the model.
  • Big Saturday moves. The largest in the data was on : of the account before the hedge, after it. A 20% Saturday at the 1.5× cap would cost about 27% before the hedge. The worst weekend overall was ().
  • The edge can fade. If more desks sell Sunday options, or vol sellers move to Saturday, it narrows. Watch the rolling 26-week Sunday result. If it is negative while Saturday is also negative, cut to 0.5×.

Live checklist for one weekend

  1. Fri eveningCheck the account value and the previous high. If below the high, compute the Saturday size: extra = gap ÷ (8 × rate), capped at 1.5×. Keep at least 50% of the account free for margin.
  2. Sat 07:55Read the index and the Sunday-expiry ATM call and put marks. Contracts = notional ÷ ETH. Place sell limits at about mark − 1%, then walk down to mark − 2% if unfilled by 08:10.
  3. Sat 08:15Set price alerts at strike × 1.03 and strike × 0.97. Write down Δcall + Δput per contract so the hedge size is ready.
  4. Alert firesbefore Sun 06:00Once, on the hourly close: hedge = (Δcall + Δput) × contracts. Positive means buy perp, negative means sell perp. Don't adjust it later.
  5. Sun 06:00Close the perp hedge at market.
  6. Sun 08:00The Saturday straddle settles automatically. Then buy the Monday-expiry ATM call and put: contracts = account ÷ ETH, limit at about mark + 1%.
  7. Mon 08:00The Sunday straddle settles. Log the fill prices vs mark, the hedge, and the P&L. Update the high-water mark.
