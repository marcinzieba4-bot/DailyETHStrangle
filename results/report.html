Bottom line: if you want out of ETH, sell today

  • The options have no real edge after costs. Since 2021 a daily ATM straddle paid 2.79% of spot per day and the 08:00-to-08:00 move cost 2.61%. After selling at the bid and paying fees, what is left is +0.02% per day, about 1.4% of the premium.
  • So the result is decided by the ETH you keep while waiting. If ETH rises first, you are out 1–2% above today's price. If it falls first, you keep a falling position, and with the put leg you double it.
  • Calls only (variant A) is a paid limit order. Over 1,880 historical start days it beat selling on the day 76% of the time, with a median gain of +1.1%. Its worst 5% of starts ended at −6.7% or worse, and its worst 1% at −17.4% or worse.
  • The put leg ("add new wstETH") is where the damage comes from. With it (variant B) the worst 5% of starts ended at −18.9% or worse, and the worst single start at −69.0%. Without a cap (variant C), the worst 1% ended at −231% or worse, a loss larger than the whole position.

Why there is no free lunch here

Daily ETH options are not cheap to sell into. Weekend days are already priced lower by the market, and the realised 08:00 move has been about 93% of what the option paid before costs (a 6.9% gross edge). Selling at the bid and paying 0.03% per leg removes most of that. The seller won on 63% of days, but the worst single day cost 21.5% of spot.

How to use this

  1. If you need to be out of ETH, sell the 50 wstETH now. That locks $125,411. Every number on this page is measured against it.
  2. If you want to be paid to wait a day or two, run A with the stop and nothing else. Sell 62.45 ETH of the next daily ATM call. On the first up settlement, short 62.45 ETH-PERP, then sell the wstETH and close the perp. If any settlement is at or below $1,909, sell everything. Historically this was flat within 3 days 90% of the time, with a median of +1.1% (about +$1,370 today). The worst 5% was −6.2% (about −$7,797) and the worst 1% was −12.1%. The stop fired in 17% of starts.
  3. To shrink the tail further, split it. Sell half now and run A on the other half. That halves both the median gain and the tail loss.
  4. Do not sell the put while you are trying to exit. Only sell puts if you would be glad to own 124.9 ETH at that strike and have the dollars to pay for it.
  5. Execution matters more than the strategy. Post orders at mid as a maker rather than hitting the bid (worth about +0.1% to +0.3% per exit). Hedge exactly at the 08:00 UTC settlement. Deribit ETH options pay premium in ETH, so convert it to USD as you go. A short perp against wstETH held elsewhere needs margin on the exchange: a 30% rally costs about $38,000 on the perp before the wstETH gain can be moved over.
  6. Mind the current regime. Daily IV (61.0) is above DVOL (56.1), so premiums are rich, but ETH is −10% in four days. Historically, starts after a drop like this had a worst 5% of −12.1% for A (versus −6.0% otherwise) and −24.9% for B. Today is a worse-than-average day to start.

Assumptions and limits

Position: 50 wstETH × 1.2491 = 62.45 ETH; options and hedges sized in ETH. One daily expiry per day at 08:00 UTC, strike = settlement price, Black–Scholes pricing. IV = DVOL × weekday factor (Mon–Thu 1.00, Fri 0.92, Sat 0.60, Sun 0.85, shaped on tonight's Deribit term structure); day one uses the live 61.0% ATM IV. Options sold 3.5% below mark, fee 0.03% of underlying per leg (capped at 12.5% of premium). Perp hedge 0.03% per change; shorts receive 1.5% APR funding in simulations and actual monthly funding in the backtest. Staking 2.33% APR (backtest uses the historical Lido rate by year). Extra wstETH costs 0.03% to buy; the final sale costs 0.05%. No yield on USD on either side. Monte Carlo resamples ETH's 2021–2026 daily 08:00 moves by weekday, scaled to each scenario's vol, so fat tails and quiet weekends are kept.

Not modelled: taxes, gas, margin calls or liquidation, wstETH discount to ETH, exchange risk, and moves between settlements (the hedge is placed only at 08:00). This is a simulation of your rules, not financial advice.
