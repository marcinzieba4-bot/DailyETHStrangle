Monthly ladder of yearly calls on 50 ETH

Every month on the 12th, sell a 1-year ATM call on 4.17 ETH (50 ÷ 12). In the first year coverage builds from 1/12 to 12/12. From then on, each month one call expires and a new one is sold at the current price. If the expiring call ended in the money, you pay the difference in cash and keep the ETH. If it ended out of the money, you keep the whole premium. The ETH is never sold. Calls still open at the end are bought back at their value that day; that is the fair cost of stopping. Their value is priced with the volatility the premium implies: 66% for 26%, 51% for 20%.

How to use this

If you exit with weekly calls

  1. Set the strike to the higher of ATM (or +5%) and $2,000. This one rule decides whether the ladder sells high or sells into dips.
  2. Pick the size by how fast you want out. At 2 ETH a week, expect well over a year even if ETH holds up. 5 ETH a week roughly doubles the pace.
  3. Treat the unsold ETH as still at risk. In a slide you keep holding, and the result is close to just holding. Decide in advance at what price you would sell the rest outright.
  4. Hedge exactly at the Friday 08:00 UTC settlement, and only when the call ends in the money. Short 2 ETH-PERP or sell 2 ETH of wstETH; the economics are the same, but the perp keeps staking running and needs margin.
  5. Sell the calls at mid where you can. Weekly ATM spreads are about 0.001 ETH, roughly 3% of the premium.

If you run the 1 ETH a day plan

  1. Treat it as holding ETH with a range-trading overlay, not as an exit. It pays when ETH keeps coming back to about $2,000 and costs you when ETH trends away.
  2. Keep the $2,000 floor. It stops the plan from selling the rebound after a dip, which was the main flaw of the version without it.
  3. Hold USD in reserve: roughly $35–40k per 1 ETH a day of lot size, and up to $75–125k in a bad stretch. In a 2022-style slide, plan for %%R_22_CASH%% (up to %%R_22_CASH95%%).
  4. Keep margin for the perp shorts on the exchange. In a rally they lose cash every day while your wstETH gains are on-chain.
  5. Scale only as far as your cash allows. 2–3 ETH a day multiplies both the range gain and the tail roughly in proportion.
  6. Execution: post at mid as a maker rather than hitting the bid. Trade at the 08:00 UTC settlement and convert the ETH-paid premium to USD.

If you want out

  1. Sell the 50 wstETH now. That locks %%SELL%%.
  2. Or get paid to wait a day or two with calls only and a stop. Sell %%Q0%% ETH of the next daily ATM call. On the first up settlement, hedge and sell. If any settlement is at or below %%STOP_PX%%, sell everything. Historically the median was %%AS_MED%% (about %%AS_MED_USD%%) and the worst 5% was %%AS_P5%% (about %%AS_P5_USD%%).
  3. Today is a worse-than-average day to start. ETH is %%RET4_NOW%% in four days. Starts after drops like this had a worst 5% of %%A_DROP_P5%% for the quick exit, against %%A_CALM_P5%% otherwise.

Assumptions and limits

Position: 50 wstETH × %%RATIO%% = %%Q0%% ETH; options and hedges sized in ETH. One daily expiry per day at 08:00 UTC, strike = settlement price, Black–Scholes pricing. IV = DVOL × weekday factor (Mon–Thu 1.00, Fri 0.92, Sat 0.60, Sun 0.85, shaped on tonight's Deribit term structure); day one uses the live %%DAILY_IV%%% ATM IV. Options sold 3.5% below mark, fee 0.03% of underlying per leg (capped at 12.5% of premium). Perp hedge 0.03% per change; shorts receive 1.5% APR funding in simulations and actual monthly funding in the backtest. Staking %%STAKE%%% APR (backtest uses the historical Lido rate by year). Extra wstETH costs 0.03% to buy; the final sale costs 0.05%. No yield on USD on either side. Monte Carlo resamples ETH's 2021–2026 daily 08:00 moves by weekday, scaled to each scenario's vol, so fat tails and quiet weekends are kept.

Not modelled: taxes, gas, margin calls or liquidation, wstETH discount to ETH, exchange risk, and moves between settlements (the hedge is placed only at 08:00). This is a simulation of your rules, not financial advice.

Code and data: repository DailyETHStrangle, branch claude/fervent-rubin-l6ifst. Rerun with python3 run_all.py --refresh then python3 report/build_report.py.
